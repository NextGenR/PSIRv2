Republic of Caste 🎓 Anand Teltumbde / Suraj Yengde
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"India's democracy sits atop an informal 'republic of caste'—universal suffrage has democratized the caste idiom rather than eradicating it."
📖 Concept & Analytical Breakdown:

Constitutional equality ('one person, one vote') left social hierarchies intact. Electoral institutions did not secularize caste; caste colonized democracy, turning elections into competitive caste-coalition bargaining.

Flailing State 🎓 Lant Pritchett
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"India is a 'flailing state'—a competent technocratic 'head' formulating elite policy, while dysfunctional administrative 'limbs' fail at delivery."

Demand Polity vs. Command Polity 🎓 Lloyd I. Rudolph & Susanne H. Rudolph
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"Indian politics oscillates between a populist 'demand polity' pandering to electoral handouts, and a centralized 'command polity' imposing top-down discipline."

#17 of 20 BATCH 1 Paper 2A International Relations
Thucydides Trap 🎓 Graham T. Allison
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"When a rising power threatens to displace an established hegemon, structural stress makes catastrophic war almost inevitable."
📖 Concept & Analytical Breakdown:

Drawing from Thucydides' Peloponnesian War (Sparta fearing rising Athens), Graham Allison surveyed 16 historical cases of hegemonic transition, finding 12 ended in war. The rising power's growing entitlement collides with the ruling power's fear of decline, making minor crises escalate into systemic conflict (e.g., US–China).

#19 of 20 BATCH 1 Paper 1A Political Theory
Agonistic Pluralism 🎓 Chantal Mouffe
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"Healthy democracy does not eliminate conflict through consensus; it transforms dangerous moral 'antagonism' into constructive democratic 'agonism'."
📖 Concept & Analytical Breakdown:

Critique of liberal and deliberative democracy (Habermas, Rawls). Mouffe argues that political struggle cannot be erased by rational consensus. Instead of moralizing politics into 'good vs. evil' (antagonism between enemies to be destroyed), democracy must channel conflict into 'agonism'—legitimate clash between adversaries who respect democratic rules.

🎯 UPSC PSIR Answer Application (Paper 1A • Democratic Theory, Deliberative Democracy Critique & Radical Pluralism)
Paper 1A (Democratic Theory): 'Chantal Mouffe's "agonistic pluralism" rescues democracy from sterile technocracy, showing that vibrant polities legitimize passionate contestation rather than repressing conflict.'

✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"Border walls are not signs of sovereign strength, but theatrical spectacles masking the unavoidable erosion of state autonomy against global currents."
