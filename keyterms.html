Republic of Caste 🎓 Anand Teltumbde / Suraj Yengde (Ambedkarite Tradition)
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"India's formal constitutional democracy sits atop an informal, entrenched 'republic of caste' where universal suffrage has democratized the caste idiom rather than eradicating it."
📖 Concept & Analytical Breakdown:

India's transition to a constitutional republic established de jure political equality ('one person, one vote'), but left the de facto social-economic hierarchy of Brahminical hegemony intact. Rather than modern institutions secularizing caste, caste has colonized modern electoral structures. Democracy functions through caste coalitions, while Dalit-Bahujan assertion is violently policed whenever it challenges entrenched social capital.

Lopsided Polarity 🎓 Stephen G. Brooks & William C. Wohlforth
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"The international order is characterized neither by stable unipolarity nor balanced multipolarity, but by a structural 'lopsided polarity'—an unassailable American military primacy coexisting with a dispersed geoeconomic landscape."
📖 Concept & Analytical Breakdown:

Traditional balance-of-power theory assumes either clear hegemony or balanced multipolar competition. Brooks and Wohlforth argue that the 21st-century order has a structural asymmetry: the United States possesses a commanding technological, military, and alliance lead that no single rival can match globally, yet rising powers (China, India) possess enough regional economic heft to resist US unilateralism, resulting in an asymmetric, fragile 'lopsided' polarity.

#3 of 100 BATCH 1 Paper 1B State & Governance
Flailing State 🎓 Lant Pritchett
✍️ THE HOOKY OPENER / CATCHPHRASE
"India exemplifies a 'flailing state'—a capable, sophisticated elite 'head' directing progressive policy and jurisprudence, connected to paralyzed, dysfunctional administrative 'limbs' at the grassroots."
📖 Concept & Analytical Breakdown:

Pritchett distinguishes between a 'failed state' (where sovereignty collapses) and a 'flailing state'. In a flailing state, apex democratic institutions (Supreme Court, RBI, ISRO, Parliament) demonstrate world-class intellectual and policymaking competence, but street-level bureaucracy (policing, primary health, municipal sanitation, primary education) suffers from systemic absenteeism, corruption, and implementation failure. The principal (the state leadership) cannot guide or discipline its agents (local bureaucracy).
